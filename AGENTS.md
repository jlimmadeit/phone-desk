# Operating the iPhone

## Finish on the Home Screen

At the end of each phone task, press **Home** in Phone Desk and verify that the iPhone Home Screen is visible. The USB Home command is authorized for this step. If the user explicitly asks to leave a particular screen open, finish there instead.

## Watching videos on YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram

When you choose a video to watch or engage with, watch it through once. For Shorts, TikToks, and Reels, swipe to the next video as the clip ends or just as the opening repeats. Use the visible playback progress, ending, or return of the opening scene to time the swipe. If you catch a clip partway through and want to watch it in full, let it loop and watch from the beginning. For a standard video that doesn't loop, move on when playback ends.

Watching a video counts as engagement. Pressing Like is optional. If the user gives a different instruction for the session, such as scrolling quickly through four clips, follow their requested pace. After the final video, finish on the iPhone Home Screen.
